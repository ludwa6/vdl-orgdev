## GENERAL CIRCLE
Vale da Lama, Lda.

### Purpose
To steward the ecological, economic, and human sustainability of Vale da Lama, Lda. as an integrated regenerative enterprise.

### Domain
- Overall organizational purpose and identity
- Organizational structure (creation and dissolution of circles)
- Company-wide strategy and direction
- Budget allocation between circles
- Legal, fiscal, and ownership responsibilities
- Company-wide values and culture

### Accountabilities
- Creating, dissolving, and evolving circles
- Defining interfaces between circles
- Approving annual budgets for circles
- Maintaining legal and fiscal compliance
- Representing the organization externally at strategic level
- Safeguarding regenerative principles across the organization

### Policies

**Policy: Subsidiarity**
Decisions must be taken at the lowest circle with the relevant authority.

**Policy: Non-Interference**
The General Circle may not override operational decisions of sub-circles unless a cross-circle tension exists.

**Policy: Transparency**
Budgets, strategic decisions, and governance records must be accessible to all circles.

**Policy: Regenerative Priority**
All strategic decisions must respect ecological regeneration and long-term soil health.

**Policy: Budget Approval**
Circle budgets must be proposed by Lead Links and approved annually by the General Circle.

### Sub-Circles
- Circle 1: Soil Regeneration & Fertility
- Circle 2: Organic Agricultural Production
- Circle 3: The House (Hospitality & Restaurant)

## CIRCLE 1: SOIL REGENERATION & FERTILITY
Vale da Lama, Lda.

### Purpose
To regenerate and maintain soil health, fertility, and ecosystem function across the Vale da Lama lands.

### Domain
- Soil management practices and protocols
- Composting systems and operations
- Cover cropping plans
- Water retention and management infrastructure
- Biodiversity corridors and hedgerows
- Soil testing and monitoring data
- Circle budget (within approved limits)

### Accountabilities
- Developing annual soil regeneration plans
- Managing composting operations
- Planning and implementing cover cropping rotations
- Monitoring soil health indicators
- Maintaining water retention infrastructure
- Coordinating with other circles on land use
- Maintaining records of soil interventions

### Policies

**Policy: No-Till Priority**
Tillage may only be used when explicitly required for soil restoration and must be documented.

**Policy: Organic Input Standards**
All soil inputs must be compatible with organic certification requirements.

**Policy: Cross-Circle Coordination**
Soil interventions that affect agricultural production must be coordinated with Circle 2 at least one season in advance.

**Policy: Water Conservation**
All water management decisions must prioritize water retention and aquifer recharge.

**Policy: Biodiversity Integration**
At least 10% of managed land must be dedicated to biodiversity corridors and non-productive ecosystem zones.

## CIRCLE 2: ORGANIC AGRICULTURAL PRODUCTION
Vale da Lama, Lda.

### Purpose
To produce certified organic vegetables and fruits using regenerative practices.

### Domain
- Crop plan (what crops, when, quantities)
- Agricultural plots, greenhouses, and orchards
- Organic (BIO) certification status and compliance
- Allocation of produce (internal vs external)
- Harvest scheduling and execution
- Agricultural team coordination
- Circle budget (within approved limits)

### Accountabilities
- Planning annual and seasonal crop rotations
- Managing daily agricultural operations (planting, maintenance, harvest)
- Maintaining organic certification compliance
- Supplying produce to Circle 3 (The House)
- Managing external sales of surplus produce
- Coordinating and supporting agricultural team
- Maintaining required BIO records and documentation
- Monitoring production costs and profitability

### Policies

**Policy: Internal Priority**
Produce must be allocated to Circle 3 (The House) first; only surplus may be sold externally.

**Policy: BIO Integrity**
No inputs or practices incompatible with organic certification may be used without explicit governance approval.

**Policy: Menu Coordination**
The crop plan must be shared with Circle 3 for input before finalization each quarter.

**Policy: Transparency on Availability**
Production availability and shortages must be communicated to Circle 3 weekly.

**Policy: Mandatory Documentation**
All activities relevant to organic certification must be properly recorded within 48 hours.

**Policy: Soil Alignment**
Agricultural practices must align with Circle 1's soil regeneration plans and may not degrade soil health.

### Sub-Circles (Optional/Activatable)

**Crop Planning & Rotation**
Purpose: To optimize crop selection and rotation for productivity and soil health.

**Production & Operations**
Purpose: To execute daily agricultural work efficiently and effectively.

**Organic Certification & Compliance**
Purpose: To maintain organic certification and all required documentation.

**Sales & Distribution**
Purpose: To manage surplus produce sales and client relationships.

## CIRCLE 3: THE HOUSE (Hospitality & Restaurant)
Vale da Lama, Lda.

### Purpose
To provide exceptional hospitality experiences that showcase regenerative agriculture and organic food.

### Domain
- Menu design and planning
- Restaurant operations and service
- Guest accommodation operations (Casa Vale da Lama)
- Retreat programming and coordination
- Guest experience and satisfaction
- Hospitality team coordination
- Circle budget (within approved limits)

### Accountabilities
- Planning seasonal menus aligned with available produce
- Managing daily restaurant operations
- Managing guest accommodation services
- Coordinating retreat programs and facilitators
- Maintaining hospitality service standards
- Supporting and coordinating hospitality team
- Communicating produce needs to Circle 2
- Monitoring financial performance of hospitality operations

### Policies

**Policy: Farm-to-Table Priority**
Menus must prioritize produce from Circle 2; external sourcing requires documenting unavailability from farm.

**Policy: Seasonal Menu Adaptation**
Menus must adapt to seasonal availability rather than demanding out-of-season production.

**Policy: Quarterly Menu Planning**
Menu plans for upcoming quarter must be shared with Circle 2 at least 6 weeks in advance.

**Policy: Quality Standards**
All food service must meet organic certification standards and align with regenerative values.

**Policy: Guest Education**
Guest experiences should include education about regenerative agriculture and the farm's practices.

**Policy: Waste Minimization**
Food waste must be minimized and properly composted in coordination with Circle 1.

### Sub-Circles (Optional/Activatable)

**Restaurant Operations**
Purpose: To deliver exceptional dining experiences using farm produce.

**Guest Accommodation**
Purpose: To provide comfortable, welcoming lodging aligned with regenerative values.

**Retreat Programs**
Purpose: To design and deliver transformative retreat experiences.

**Guest Relations**
Purpose: To ensure exceptional guest satisfaction and relationship building.

## Notes on Structure

### What Changed from Original
1. **Purposes cleaned** - Removed goals, outcomes, and constraints
2. **Domain clarified** - Listed specific resources under control
3. **Accountabilities separated** - Ongoing activities extracted from Purpose
4. **Policies explicit** - Constraints and rules made into formal Policies
5. **Cross-circle relationships** - Captured as Policies rather than in Purpose

### Key Holacracy Principles Applied
- **Single Purpose** - Each circle has ONE clear reason to exist
- **Domain Authority** - Resources explicitly under circle control
- **Ongoing Activities** - Accountabilities are continuous, not projects
- **Constraints as Policies** - Rules on domain use are explicit
- **Subsidiarity** - Decisions at lowest appropriate level

### Implementation in Notion
These definitions should be entered into separate databases:
- One entry per circle in **Circles** database
- Each Domain item as separate entry in **Domain Elements** database
- Each Accountability as separate entry in **Accountabilities** database
- Each Policy as separate entry in **Policies** database

This allows flexible relationships and evolution over time.
