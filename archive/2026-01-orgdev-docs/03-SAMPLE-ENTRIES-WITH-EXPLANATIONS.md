## Summary of Proper Separation

### Purpose (WHY the circle exists)
```
To produce certified organic vegetables and fruits using regenerative practices.
```

**NOT:** "To produce vegetables, ensure quality, maintain continuity, align with The House..."

### Domain (WHAT the circle controls)
- Crop Plan (the artifact)
- Agricultural Plots & Greenhouses (the assets)
- Allocation of Produce (decision authority)
- Circle Budget (financial resource)
- Organic Certification Status (relationship)

**NOT:** "Planning crops" or "Quality control" (those are accountabilities)

### Accountabilities (WHAT the circle does - ongoing "-ing" activities)
- Planning annual and seasonal crop rotations
- Managing daily agricultural operations
- Maintaining organic certification compliance
- Supplying produce to Circle 3 (The House)
- Managing external sales of surplus produce
- Coordinating and supporting agricultural team

**NOT:** Goals, outcomes, or one-time projects

### Policies (HOW domain elements must be used - constraints)
- **Internal Priority:** "Produce must be allocated to Circle 3 first; only surplus may be sold externally"
- **Menu Coordination:** "Crop plan must be shared with Circle 3 for input before finalization each quarter"
- **BIO Integrity:** "No inputs incompatible with organic certification may be used without governance approval"

**NOT:** Vague principles or restated accountabilities

---

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

❌ **Don't put accountabilities in Purpose**
- Wrong: "To produce vegetables and ensure quality"
- Right: Purpose = "To produce vegetables", Accountability = "Maintaining quality standards"

❌ **Don't make domain too vague**
- Wrong: "Quality" or "Production"
- Right: "BIO Certification Status" or "Crop Plan"

❌ **Don't make policies into accountabilities**
- Wrong: Accountability = "Following organic standards"
- Right: Accountability = "Maintaining certification", Policy = "Must use only BIO-approved inputs"

❌ **Don't nest everything in one database**
- Wrong: One "Circle" record with all text fields
- Right: Separate databases with relations (Circles, Domain Elements, Accountabilities, Policies)

---

## Testing Your Understanding

When entering a new circle, ask:
1. **Purpose:** Why does this circle exist? (One sentence)
2. **Domain:** What specific things can only this circle control?
3. **Accountabilities:** What ongoing work must this circle perform?
4. **Policies:** What rules constrain how domain elements are used?

If any answer bleeds into another category, you're mixing concerns.
