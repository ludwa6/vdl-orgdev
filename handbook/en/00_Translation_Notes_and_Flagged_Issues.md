## C. Translation decisions — for Walt and Nita to settle

None of these are errors. They are places where I had to choose, and the choice should be yours.

**C1 — The motto.** *"Da Horta ao Prato… e do Prato à Horta."* I translated it as **"From Garden to Plate… and from Plate to Garden"** in docs 01 and 02, but **kept the Portuguese** where it names the restaurant (doc 07) and where it appears as a quoted principle (docs 07, 08), with the English in brackets on first use. A motto is a brand asset and often reads better untranslated. **Decide: translate it everywhere, keep it in Portuguese everywhere, or the mixed approach used here.**

**C2 — *colaboradores*.** Translated as **"staff"** (and "team" where it clearly means the working team). "Collaborators" is a false friend in English — it suggests external partners, or worse. If "staff" feels too transactional for the culture these documents describe, **"team members"** is the natural alternative.

**C3 — *Quinta*.** Kept in Portuguese as part of the proper name **"Quinta Vale da Lama"**, and translated as **"the farm"** where the PT uses *a Quinta* generically. Consistent with keeping *Casa* untranslated.

**C4 — *Circular Lead* / *Circular Rep*.** **Kept exactly as written**, because they are already English terms chosen by the organisation — translating them would be inventing a third form.

But flagging them, because they are worth a deliberate decision: **these are not standard Holacracy terms.** Holacracy uses *Lead Link* and *Rep Link* (which the 08-03 version used); the closest current variant is *Circle Lead*, not *Circular Lead*. Two consequences:

- Anyone arriving with Holacracy experience will not recognise them, and searching for them online returns nothing.
- **"Circular" already means something else in this Handbook** — *circular economy* — and it appears in that sense in docs 01, 07 and 08. The same word now carries two unrelated meanings.

The Portuguese names, ***Líder do Círculo*** and ***Representante do Círculo***, already do the job well. The English parenthetical is the part that adds confusion. **Options: revert to Lead Link / Rep Link; use Circle Lead / Circle Rep; or keep Circular Lead / Circular Rep as a deliberate house term.** Cheap to change now, expensive after printing and training.

**C5 — British spelling** used throughout (*organisation*, *recognised*, *prioritise*). Switchable to US spelling in one pass if preferred.

**C6 — Holacracy®** — the registered mark is preserved as written in the PT.

## D. Gaps between the documents and the diagram

Not translation issues, but they surfaced while working through the set, and they affect what the English says because the English cannot invent what the Portuguese does not contain.

- **D1 — The three cross-cutting Roles exist only in the graphic.** The diagram names **HR, Marketing and Accounting** under *Roles Transversais*. **No document mentions them.** Doc 06 refers only to *"cross-cutting organisational Roles"* in the abstract. Either the diagram is ahead of the text, or the text needs to catch up.

- **D2 — Facilitator and Secretary are defined but never assigned.** Both appear in the glossary (doc 09) and in the diagram, but **no document says which Circles have them, or who appoints them.** Doc 05 makes appointing *Circular Leads* an Anchor Circle accountability; the Facilitator and Secretary are left unowned.

- **D3 — Where do Governance Meetings actually happen?** The diagram labels the **Vale da Lama Circle** as *"Reunião de Governança"*. But doc 05 gives the **Anchor Circle** authority over the Governance System, and doc 10 says the system evolves through Governance Meetings. The two readings point at different Circles.

- **D4 — The cross-cutting Roles' scope narrowed.** The old diagram said they *"support all Circles and the whole organisation"*; the new one says they *"support the whole Casa and Farm, and the whole ecosystem"*. Read literally, HR/Marketing/Accounting no longer support the Anchor and Vale da Lama Circles.

- **D5 — No Rep flows upward from the Vale da Lama Circle to the Anchor Circle.** Casa and Farm each send a Circular Lead and a Circular Rep up to the Vale da Lama Circle. The Anchor Circle sends direction down and receives nothing structural back. This is unchanged from the previous diagram, so it is not new — but in Holacracy the Rep Link exists precisely so that authority is answerable downward, and as drawn there is no formal channel by which the rest of the organisation reaches the Anchor Circle.

## E. Substantive changes since the 08-03 version — a separate conversation

The 08-10 set is a **rewrite, not a revision**: every document was re-authored, one (02, the Strategy) is entirely new, and the terminology changed throughout. Several changes alter meaning rather than wording — including a new Anchor Circle accountability covering **changes to the shareholding structure, ownership and investment** (doc 05), and a set of six smaller changes that consistently move authority upward and soften the language of Circle autonomy.

Those are governance questions rather than translation questions, and they are documented separately in the vault note *"Vale da Lama Governance Handbook v2 — What Changed and What to Raise with Nita (2026-08-11)"*. **This document deliberately stays out of that territory** so the translation review is not entangled with the governance review.
