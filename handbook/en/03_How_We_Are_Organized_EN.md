# VALE DA LAMA GOVERNANCE HANDBOOK

## 03. How We Are Organised

### Introduction

This document describes how Vale da Lama organises itself, distributes responsibilities, and makes decisions.

The Vale da Lama Governance System draws on the principles of Holacracy®, adapted to the reality of Vale da Lama, seeking to combine autonomy, clarity of responsibilities, collaboration and continuous learning.

We believe an organisation works better when decisions are made by those closest to the work, within a clear structure that is shared by everyone.

---

### Our Structure

Vale da Lama is organised into Circles.

Each Circle has a Purpose, Domains, Accountabilities and, where needed, Policies. Circles work autonomously within their area of activity and collaborate continuously with one another to fulfil the Purpose of Vale da Lama.

This structure makes it possible to distribute leadership, clarify responsibilities, and foster a more agile, transparent and resilient organisation.

### What is a Circle?

A Circle is a permanent team responsible for a specific area of the organisation.

Each Circle has:

- **Purpose** — why it exists.
- **Domains** — what it has the authority to decide on.
- **Accountabilities** — what it is answerable for.
- **Policies** — rules or principles that guide how it acts, where needed.
- **Roles** — the specific functions that carry out the Circle's work.

Each Circle has the autonomy to make decisions within its Domains, collaborating with the other Circles to fulfil the Purpose of Vale da Lama.

### Our Circles

**Anchor Circle**
Protects the future of Vale da Lama, preserving the Purpose, the Values, and the evolution of the organisation's governance, and defines the Strategy of Vale da Lama.

**Operations Circle**
Implements the Strategy of Vale da Lama and directs the day-to-day operations of the organisation, fostering alignment, collaboration and support between the different Circles.

**Casa Circle**
Cares for the hospitality of Vale da Lama, providing authentic experiences to guests and visitors.

**Regenerative Farm Circle**
Cares for the land, produces organic food, and develops the farm's regenerative systems.

### Lama Village

Lama Village is a resident community connected to Vale da Lama.

Its relationship with the organisation develops in its own way, following the evolution of the community and of Vale da Lama itself.

### Where can I find out more?
